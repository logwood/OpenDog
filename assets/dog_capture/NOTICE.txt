Dog face localizer and 46-point landmark weights:
Author: Hugo Cornellier; training data: DogFLW, Tech4Animals Lab.
Source: https://huggingface.co/hugocornellier/dog-face-landmarks
Revision: ffe69649279e0c226e7061f917550ca8608abbfe
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International

Modification: expose existing heatmap probabilities as an additional output.
Weights and the original coordinate output computation are unchanged.
The runtime derivative remains under CC BY-NC 4.0.

v2: reduce landmark probability maps to 46 entropy values on the inference
device. v3: export to ONNX/TensorRT FP16, preserving FP32 coordinate/entropy
reductions; use contiguous spatial softmax and the original coordinate grid.
Weights remain unchanged. Floating-point backend outputs are compared in
the validation report and are not claimed to be bitwise identical.
The EfficientDet TensorRT derivative dequantizes INT8 weights and removes
activation quantization; box decoding/class reduction run on the GPU.
TensorRT/CUDA runtimes are installed from NVIDIA and are not bundled here.

DogFLW reference: Martvel et al. (2025), Dog facial landmarks detection and
its applications for facial analysis. Scientific Reports 15, 21886.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-07040-3

EfficientDet Lite0: Google / TensorFlow Lite official object detection model.
https://www.tensorflow.org/lite/examples/object_detection/overview
LiteRT runtime: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/LiteRT (Apache-2.0).

No system DLL is bundled. Windows WebGPU loads an available Microsoft DXC
runtime; the optional official distribution is at:
https://github.com/microsoft/DirectXShaderCompiler/releases
